# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and deliberate disruption of
discussions. Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/emil-eklund/minne/security/advisories/new)
or by email. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit, delete or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that do not follow this document, and may ban repeat offenders from the project.
